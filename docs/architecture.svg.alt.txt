OperatorOS Platform v1.0 architecture diagram (alt text).

The diagram is a layered composition rendered on a dark navy background. The title at the top reads "OperatorOS Platform v1.0 — Architecture" with a subtitle that names the frozen architecture SHA-256 ("1e79049d9ae5a328556378ff8235525cd0f692bfa317fd7da6dc2bcdb1f27610"), the count of authoritative components (4), and the count of integration packages (9).

The layout is read bottom-up. The bottom layer is "Contracts" — a single wide rounded panel that represents the public contract vocabulary package (@operatoros-platform/contracts). It sits visually below the rest of the platform, with three small grey arrows pointing up from its top edge labelled "depends on", showing that the three authoritative services are built on top of the contract vocabulary.

The middle layer is the authoritative services triangle. Three rounded panels of equal size sit side by side. On the left is "Evidence Service" (cyan outline), the evidence ledger that appends immutable Event Records and seals Mission Records. In the centre is "Workspace Service", which validates and commits Workspace-scoped mutations and rebuilds projections. On the right is "Execution Service", the 8-state Run state machine with optimistic concurrency and checkpoint/recovery fences. Cyan arrows show that Workspace Service depends on Evidence Service, Execution Service depends on Workspace Service, and there are dashed cyan arrows indicating cross-references from the Execution Service back down to Evidence and Workspace for run-state coordination.

The top layer is the perimeter of integration packages, drawn as smaller rounded panels arrayed around the upper edges of the diagram. The top-left to top-right sequence is: Interface Host, Governance, Agent Execution, Extension Runtime, Hosted Runtime, and v08-importer. The lower-perimeter row contains: Recovery, Secrets, and Distributed Coordination. Each integration panel is connected with dashed cyan arrows down to the authoritative service(s) it integrates with: Interface Host points to Workspace and Execution; Governance points to Workspace and Execution; Agent Execution points to Execution and Workspace; Extension Runtime points to Workspace and Execution; Hosted Runtime points to Execution and Workspace; v08-importer points to Workspace (read-only); Recovery points to Execution and Evidence; Secrets points to Workspace and Execution; and Distributed Coordination points to Evidence and Execution.

A legend at the bottom-left decodes the line styles: solid cyan arrows mean integration wires, dashed cyan arrows mean dependency, and grey arrows mean a service depends on the contracts layer. A small status badge at the bottom-right records the v1.0 release evidence: 13 packages, 146 tests, 8 authorities locked.
